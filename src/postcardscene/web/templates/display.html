Panel power policy

Automatic tries usable capabilities in fixed priority: HDMI-CEC → DDC/CI → signal. Explicit backend selection is strict and does not silently fall through.

Signal only can confirm output signal power, but may not prove physical TV or monitor standby.

Wake delay applies after an actual wake transition before presentation release. Saving sends no runtime command; the runtime reads policy on its bounded reconciliation cadence.

Diagnostic tests

Tests are temporary diagnostics for five seconds, not scheduling or manual overrides. They change no saved settings or schedule. The runtime automatically returns to current policy after five seconds.

Test wake may temporarily override ordinary operating sleep, but never protection sleep. Backend unavailability may prevent a transition even when a test is submitted.
